Local by design

Privacy Policy

Darts Checkout Coach UK · Last updated 18 August 2026

No account. No tracking. No network sync.

The app is a local scoring and practice tool. Your match and checkout records stay on the device where you enter them.

No data collection

Darts Checkout Coach UK does not collect personal data, create user accounts, run analytics, show ads, or track you. It does not send your darts data over a network.

Stored on this device

Player names you enter, completed legs, practice attempts, app settings, and unfinished match or practice drafts are stored locally with AsyncStorage on this device.

Draft expiry

Unfinished match and practice drafts expire after 24 hours. Expired drafts are removed instead of reopening a stale board.

Your control

You can remove saved legs, practice history, drafts, and settings at any time with Clear Local Data in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device platform's backup behaviour.
