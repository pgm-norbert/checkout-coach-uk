Scoring a leg

From Home, tap 501 or 301, choose who throws first, then enter each visit total and tap Add Visit. When a player reaches zero, confirm whether the checkout took one, two or three darts.

Checkout routes

As soon as a finish is on, the board shows a suggested route. Checkout Practice serves finishes from 40 to 170; mark each attempt Checked Out or Missed.

Clearing your data

Settings has a Clear Local Data control. Everything is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
